### FND.2760 [medium] [contradiction] - POS.0120 says a position is dated once and carries no measurement; a third of the positions carry two to four dates and several carry counts and trials
- **Location:** 10-intent.md POS.0120 ("it is dated once ... Dated
  once means one date, the day the position took its present shape;
  earlier steps are found by searching the history ... A number stays
  only where it is the rule or a threshold, never as a measurement";
  "What does not belong in it is the way to it: why it was changed,
  what was said on the way, trials, measurements, counts") against,
  for the dates, POS.0220 ("Decided 2026-10-17; kept 2026-10-23"),
  POS.0550 ("Decided 2026-10-22", "decided 2026-10-23", "decided
  2026-10-21"), POS.0800 (2026-08-17, 2026-10-23, 2026-10-24,
  "Reworded 2026-10-24"), POS.0850 ("Decided 2026-09-27, `question`
  added 2026-10-13, the steps outside a walkthrough 2026-10-24"),
  POS.0930, POS.1200 ("Decided 2026-09-21 and 2026-10-21 ...; the ask
  2026-10-23"), POS.1560, POS.1690, POS.1700, POS.1710, POS.1750,
  POS.1770; for the measurements, POS.1870 ("four of six notes of one
  engine rewritten from the other's"), POS.1890 ("inventing or
  misnumbering three of fourteen sources in ten minutes"), POS.1450
  ("79 pages"), POS.0220 ("stand past 1700 after two months"),
  POS.1780 ("three times"), POS.0850 ("three files were written on
  it").
- **Issue:** The rule and the practice cannot both hold. A date per
  clause records the way to the position, which the same rule sends
  to the history; a count of notes or of sources is a measurement
  from a trial, which the rule excludes in words. FND.0400 fixed the
  dates at 3.48 and seven positions were aligned; every position
  named above was born or rewritten after that, and the drift is now
  wider than it was then.
- **Why it matters:** POS.0120 is the one place that says what a
  position may carry; a reader writing the next position has two
  models in front of him, the rule and thirty positions that break it,
  and will follow the thirty. The history, which POS.0310 makes the
  single primary of the way to an item, then holds less than the
  positions do.
- **Suggested fix:** One of two, decided once: either each position
  named keeps the date of its present shape alone and the earlier
  dates and the counts go to its history at the next write that
  touches it, `Was` carrying the words; or POS.0120 is reworded to
  what is now wanted ("a position names the day of its present shape
  and may name the day of each part that was decided apart; a count
  stays where it is the reason the position was taken, never as a
  measurement of a trial") and the positions stand. The second is the
  smaller write; which one is the principal's.
