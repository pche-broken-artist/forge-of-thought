### FND.2730 [medium] [shifted] — The brief wants a deep research on two families; the intent makes the second family an option asked for by name
- **Location:** 10-intent.md POS.1860 ("`--deep` is the plan on the session model, one isolated agent per card") and POS.1870 ("A deep research *may* run on a second engine beside the session model, `/research --deep --engine <name>`"); against 00-brief-research.md v0.3, "What I want" ("I want the same plan run on two model families, Gemini beside Claude, both doing the full work on every question ... Before I confirm the plan, the second model reads the draft and says what it misses") and "How I want it built" ("`/research --deep` for the plan on two engines with a synthesis"); the mining record of 5.37; the 0.3 record of the brief's history.
- **Issue:** In the brief, two families are what a deep research *is*: the 0.3 record of the brief's own history defines quick as the depth that "drops the second engine, the plan review and the synthesis", which leaves deep with all three. In the intent, deep is the session model alone, and the second engine, its reading of the draft plan and the side-by-side answers come only when the principal names an engine at the call. The default moved from two families to one. No REJ, no DEC and no clause of the mining record says so; POS.1870's paragraph on the quota as a budget of the day is the nearest thing to a reason, and it does not say that the default was narrowed because of it. The definition (`/research`, "only with `--engine`") follows the intent, not the brief.
- **Why it matters:** The brief's one sentence on why it wants two families ("One model alone is narrower than the web it searches") is the brief's core; under the intent as written, every deep research run without the extra word is exactly the one-family research the brief set out to replace. A reader measuring drift against brief 0.3, as POS.0110 asks, finds the default reversed with nothing to tell him whether it was meant.
- **Suggested fix:** If the narrowing is the principal's: say in POS.1860 or POS.1870 that the second engine is asked for by name and why (the quota of the day, the engine unusable, the cost), and add that clause to the mining record of 5.37 or a REJ for "two engines as the default of `--deep`"; then bring the brief's "How I want it built" to it. If it is not: make `--deep` the two-family research the brief describes and `--engine` the choice of which family, in the intent first and the skill after it.
