# Collaboration cutover

The building of the operating layer for the brief `collaboration`. The
preparation and the first release were built on main in one stretch and
released on 2026-10-19 (5.26; DEC.0230, DEC.0250, DEC.0260); every
project was migrated the same day. The second, third and fourth release
were deferred (DEC.0250, THR.0710) and let go on 2026-10-23 by the
principal's word (brief 0.9, Principle): their rows stay below as
dropped, their ids addresses as before. What is built instead is the
simplified second release at the end: the conflict step, the hook's ask,
the rules of ID and version after a merge. The way and the why:
`work/collaboration-cutover.spec.md`. States: todo, doing, done,
dropped. The order of the rows is the order of building; an id is an
address, keeps the prefix of the release it was born in and does not
change when a row moves. Worked by hand since 2026-10-17
(`sources/cutover-plan-critique.md`, `sources/cutover-challenge-
review.md`, DEC.0230, `sources/cutover-challenge-review-2.md`, DEC.0240,
DEC.0250; `sources/collaboration-simplification-challenge.md`, brief
0.9). Dead after R6.07; it stays as it lies.
