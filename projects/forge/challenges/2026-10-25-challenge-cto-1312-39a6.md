# Peer review (cto) — 2026-10-25

## Overall read

At 5.37 the intent is the design record of an engine that has, in the fifteen days since its major, taken on a merge and migration model, a hook that arbitrates git verb by verb, a BRD layer with its own persona and check, a research command that plans first and runs on two model families, a working folder, a work-product kind, a video player and a proposed third kind of project: thirty-seven minors and 278 history records. Most of it is well reasoned and much of it was tested before it was written down (POS.1780), which is more than most engineering organisations manage. What I cannot find is the thing the forge exists for moving at the same pace: no subject chain has changed by content since 5.0, the one new lower-layer artefact is an automatic draft nobody has judged, the brief that was to frame the whole has waited since 3 October while five briefs born after it were mined past it, and the home the last review's rollout questions were sent to is being redefined as a kind of project that has no intent. The forge is getting better at being the forge faster than anyone is finding out whether it works on anything else.

## Challenges

### CHL.0620 — The concept brief is being answered by accretion while it waits, and the parked challenge about exactly that fell due at 4.0 and again at 5.0
- **Severity:** major
- **Challenge:** `next-gen` (0.4, pending) is, in the principal's own words, the brief that is "to make us think of the whole as a concept", with "whether we are not heading somewhere that cannot be managed" in its first section. Since it was born on 2026-10-03, the briefs `documentation`, `collaboration`, `brd` and `research` were born after it and mined, and each decided a piece of the whole it was to frame: upgrade and compatibility became the migration (POS.1740) without the format version or the stable line; automation became handing over (POS.1410, POS.1810) and a second engine (POS.1870); the shape of the whole gained a `workshop` kind by thread (THR.0750); user modifications and the engine split stay open (THR.0300, THR.0230, THR.0190). CHL.0150, "THR.0230 is being decided by accretion, in the direction that makes the later decision most expensive", was parked on 2026-09-06 "until after 4.0"; 4.0 came, 5.0 came, and it is still parked. The measure the brief asked for has an answer meanwhile: CLAUDE.md 523 lines on 2026-09-03, 650 on 2026-10-03, 815 on 2026-10-22, 853 today; the intent 2 363 lines on 2026-10-01, 2 532 on 2026-10-22, 2 763 today; 27 scripts, 30 skills, 13 agents, 96 documentation pages. The principal's word of 2026-10-22, that the brief is to be worked through again and better before it is mined, is the right instinct; nothing in the record slows the accretion while he does.
- **Why it matters:** Every position written before the concept narrows what the concept can still decide, and the ones written since 5.0 are the expensive kind: a migration mechanism, a merge model, a hook with a verb table, a second-engine link with its own profile scheme. If `next-gen` concludes that the core should be smaller, or that a plugin is the only shape a locked-down company allows, each of these is a thing to undo, not a thing to add. "Held loosely" is how the brief describes its own order; the record shows what loosely costs.
- **What would change my mind:** Either a stated rule for 5.x that new positions outside the open threads of the trunk wait until `next-gen` is mined, with the exceptions named; or a decision, recorded as such, that the piecemeal path is the chosen one and `next-gen` is reduced to what the other briefs did not take. Either closes CHL.0150 honestly.
- **Epistemic status:** my judgement; the dates, counts and the parking read from the files.

### CHL.0630 — The evidence of use is thinner than the evidence of self-change, and the home the rollout's questions were sent to is dissolving
- **Severity:** major

On the other side of the ledger: `agentic-platform`'s assignment stands at 1.3 since September and its intent's last content change is 2026-10-14, bookkeeping; `flow-ba`'s assignment at 0.4 and intent at 0.10, last touched to close findings; the one new lower-layer artefact anywhere, the BRD of `agentic-platform` at 0.1, was drafted automatically on 2026-10-23 with roughly 125 items (counted from its created record) and has no record since. The one external signal is good and real: a user ran the scripts on Linux, opened an issue and sent a pull request (POS.0830, POS.0550). It is a signal about the scripts, not about the chain.
- **Why it matters:** POS.0780 keeps outcomes outside the forge's sight by design, which was the right answer for a subject project and is no answer for the engine's own development: the engine's feedback loop is the principal's use, and his use is of the engine on itself. The failure mode nobody would admit in twelve months is an engine that is complete, documented, portable, tested on a fixture and has produced one approved assignment, in September.

### CHL.0640 — The intent-solution cut did not survive velocity: the intent is again the chronicle and now also the specification
- **Severity:** major
- **Challenge:** POS.1390 gives the test: a sentence is intent if it would still hold were the thing realised in a wholly different way. Applied to positions born or rewritten since 5.0: POS.1200 lists the git verbs and flags the hook allows, asks and denies, and names the file; POS.1710 says what `forge-save` does with a rebase that conflicts; POS.1740 lists a migration script's refusal conditions and what it undoes from the index; POS.1880 names the script, the table of engines, the copied settings profile, the tree comparison and the first engine's row; POS.1890 says a script reads an arXiv page for its title; POS.1870 says the first engine's notes are held outside the bundle until the second's return. None of these would hold under a different realisation; all of them are the realisation. Meanwhile the solution design at 0.45 still says in its head that the principal has not judged it whole and that the forge cannot be built from its artefacts alone, which is what POS.1400 asks of a chain. CHL.0180, "the intent has become the chronicle it was designed not to be", was accepted on 2026-09-06; the intent has grown by well over a thousand lines since, 230 of them in the last three days. The design is a second copy nobody reads whole; the file is the truth; the intent is where the author actually reads, which is why the mechanism lands there.
- **Why it matters:** Second-order effect on cost: a change of the operating layer is now written into the intent, the design, the file and two histories, and regenerates the documentation whole because every page cites the intent as one hash (noted in `next-gen` and not thought through). The rational adaptation under that load is exactly what the record shows: the design unjudged, positions carrying the mechanism, a thread (THR.0640) asking whether to split the files. Second-order effect on the forge as a product: a 2 763-line intent is a bus factor of one; a second maintainer, which the public project and CONTRIBUTING invite, cannot find what holds without the author.
- **What would change my mind:** The POS.1390 test applied to the positions born or rewritten since 5.0, with what fails it moved down and the design judged; or an honest decision that for the forge's own project the intent may carry the solution and the design is folded back, the test then saying the design layer is for subject projects. The second is cheaper and may be truer; the one thing that does not work is both documents, one of them unread.
- **Epistemic status:** my judgement on the reading of the test; the head of the design and the line counts verified.

### CHL.0650 — The walls are verified by hand on single days, the surface they guard tripled since 5.0, and the accepted test of behaviour has nothing scheduled
- **Severity:** major

Since then the forge has added behaviour that is claimed as a wall rather than as conduct: the git hook's ask and deny (POS.1200), "tried 2026-10-21, seen 2026-10-23", in one permission mode; the second engine's per-run profile with the forge's tree and the shell denied and read-only "verified by comparing the folder before and after" (POS.1880), by probes on 2026-10-24; the refusals of a release on a repository behind or diverged (POS.1710). ### CHL.0670 — The check roster now polices the forge's own boilerplate, and the decision in front of the principal is to automate the policing
- **Severity:** minor
- **Challenge:** Of the 224 findings since 2026-10-02 (FND.0440 to FND.2670), roughly 170 are check findings, the bulk of them light-check conformance (counted from the ledger's table): the template's comment put into sixteen history companions, a Waiting line that names the wrong recipe version, an empty line between a comment and the first record, a header date, the same resolution text pasted into fourteen findings (FND.2090 to FND.2220). The check runs at every save, cost 131 to 160 seconds in the last measurement, and a walkthrough. The principal's own rule, POS.1480, says that when a rule has failed the question is what kind of content slipped through; here the question not asked is what kind of defect a stale template comment causes for any reader. THR.0530 proposes to turn the light check into a program, with a decision on 2026-10-25, which would cement the clauses as they stand. The forge has already built the rational adaptation to the load: `/save --quick`.
- **Why it matters:** A conformance check that mostly finds boilerplate trains its user to skip it, and then the one clause that matters, an unparsable file, a thread cited that does not exist, is skipped with the rest. Scripting the check before pruning it makes the noise free and permanent.
- **What would change my mind:** A reading of the last sixty light findings by what each protected for a reader of the project; if most protected nothing, the clauses cut before the program is written. The program for the remainder is then the right call.
- **Epistemic status:** my judgement; the counts from the ledger, the duration from THR.0410.
