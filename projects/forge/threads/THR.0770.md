A release that runs on its own: `/release --auto`, with an agreed
line between what it decides itself and where it stops. The
principal's word of 2026-11-01: the release is one of the forge's
biggest chores. Opened 2026-11-01.

The sample is the release of intent 6.4, run on the order
"autonomously, stop only at serious things". Claude's draft, shown
the same day, the principal agreeing with three corrections:
- stops always: the repository behind or diverged from its origin;
  the tag of an approved major, which is a deliberate act;
- runs on its own: the checks and every run of agents, the model
  and the size said as information, not asked; the documentation,
  which belongs to the release (the principal's correction: the
  sample stopped before `/document` to have the writers approved,
  and should not have);
- fixes on its own and records: the findings that are a plain fix
  of wording and the immediate fixes; stops at a finding that would
  change a position or a rule, and at severity high;
- the renders taken without the principal ruling on the delta,
  stopping only when the scan finds an instance fact the released
  version did not carry;
- the commit message drafted from the history and used;
- the critique `essence` not run.

What the sample showed besides: the light check ran twice, once in
the release's own step and again inside `/save`; a re-wrap of the
documentation contract, wording unchanged, changed the generator's
hash and remade all 101 pages; the README rendered before
`/document` read the documentation index of the version before and
was rendered again after it. Open: the walk of the rows one by one,
then one write of a position, the skill `release` and the row of
the Commands table.

Closed 2026-11-01, the day it was opened, after a walkthrough of six
items: the boundary of the findings `--auto` fixes and where it
stops; the renders taken without a ruling, the one stop a new
instance fact; the drafted message used; the order of every
release, documentation before the README, and the save without a
second `light` check; the generator's hash with its whitespace
normalised, so that a re-wrap regenerates nothing; `--auto` as a
sentence of POS.1100, the Commands row, `critique essence` not run,
the tag of a major never automatic. Written at intent 6.7, design
0.51, the release skill, `scripts/docs-state.py`.
