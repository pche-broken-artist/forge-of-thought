A record of what was sent out of a project, pinned to the exact
version. A visitor asks (discussion #6, 2026-11-02) for a
project-level record of every sending out of the forge: which file,
made from what, to whom, by which channel and when, each entry pinned
to the commit that holds the file, since a render is overwritten by
the next `/render` and a commit hash or a hand-set tag says nothing of
where the file went. His sketch: `sent.md` in the project root,
append-only, one line per sending, written on the principal's word
only, born at the first sending; his two questions: whether a sending
also sets a tag, and whether an entry belongs in the ledger, say to
flag that a render sent out has since gone stale.

What the forge has today: the companion of a published file carries
the provenance of that file (`/publish` step 6), a render's
front-matter its own, and the Published table says current or stale;
no record says that anything left the project, to whom or when. Open:
whether the record is a file of its own, as sketched, or a record of
state in the recipe's history, where the forge keeps what happened to
a document (POS.1610); whether a work product and a published file
are sent the same way; the two questions above. Related: the delivery
side (THR.0140), wider and not worked on. Opened 2026-11-08 from the
discussion; nothing decided.
