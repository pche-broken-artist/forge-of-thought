What of a thread survives its closing. A thread
was a small open matter and has grown: 35 threads, about 12 900
words on 2026-10-11, the five largest 900 to 1 500 words each and
plans with step tables, syntheses and measurements rather than a
question to elicit (THR.0470, THR.0230, THR.0520, THR.0630,
THR.0620). The rule that a settled thread leaves `threads.md` with
its working debate, its wording in git, was set provisionally at
4.37 ("until step 3 a closed thread leaves as before") and never
revisited; a closed thread left one `closed` record in the history
(THR.0490 at 5.1). The principal's word of 2026-10-11: not sure the
disappearance is right; some of it should rather be a brief, but
not all; and a thread holds open work well, one finds one's way in
it. Claude's options, offered once: the `closed` record carries
what was weighed and dropped, more than a sentence; a thread that
has become a plan is born as a brief; an archive of closed threads,
which he advised against as a new document kind. Decided at 5.4:
the `closed` record carries the thread's last text in `Was`
(POS.0310). Left: when a thread has become a plan and is rather
born as a brief. From THR.0470, closed at 5.5: whether large files
are split under a master index; the intent has 1 845 lines and the
threads 1 255 on 2026-10-11. Opened 2026-10-11.

The `Was` question settled 2026-10-17: a closed thread stays as its
file, immutable, and carries no `Was` (POS.1680). Open stay the two
questions: when a thread that has become a plan is rather born as a
brief, and whether large files are split.

State 2026-10-22: the first question answered by practice in the
walk of the threads: a thread that has become a plan goes to a brief
where it is thinking to be sifted (THR.0620 into the brief next-gen)
and to a work product where it is work to be done (the cutover plan,
POS.1520; the tests of THR.0650 into it), and a thread stays a
question (POS.0920). Three cases of one day, not yet a position.
Open stays whether large files are split: 41 thread files and an
intent of 2 532 lines on 2026-10-22.

2026-10-25: the cto challenge (CHL.0640, parked here until 6.x) reads
the growth of the intent as the cut of POS.1390 not surviving velocity:
positions since 5.0 carry the mechanism. Two ways named: the test
applied and the mechanism moved down, or the design folded back into the
intent for the forge's own project. The principal leans to the first:
the positions since 5.0 gone through by the test and cut, rather than
the present state accepted. The split of large files asked above is one
answer to the same pressure.
