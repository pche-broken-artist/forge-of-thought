The notation `shall` / `shall not` sits awkwardly inside the
sentences of an artefact in Czech, and either the rule or the
render must change. CLAUDE.md, prime directive 6, makes English the
notation throughout, `shall` among it, and the Requirement style of
the assignment and of the BRD builds on it; in a Czech BRD that
gives "CMS shall editorovi umožnit…", which the stakeholders read
badly. Found in the trial of the user-stories layer on a small
project, where the principal kept `shall` for now as the rule has
it. What the thread must settle: whether an artefact in a language
other than English may carry that language's modal ("musí /
nesmí") instead, and what the checks and the critic then read as
the marker of a requirement; or whether `shall` stays and a render
in the project's language turns it into the modal. Claude's view,
offered once: changing the rule is cheaper than a translation at
every render and two wordings of one requirement; not verified how
many places depend on the word. To take after the trial, with the
position of the user-stories layer. Opened 2026-11-02.

Closed 2026-11-08: the rule is one of kind, an obligation in the
indicative, never a conditional, a permission or a wish, never MoSCoW
(POS.0250). In English strictly shall / shall not, the group's
standard; an artefact in another language uses its own verb of
obligation and the forge names no word of another language. The render
that would translate `shall` is REJ.0540. THR.0540, the same question
raised 2026-10-02, closed with it (intent 6.20).
