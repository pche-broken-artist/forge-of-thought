A question put to the manual: `/man <free
text>`. The principal's idea of 2026-10-15: one wants to do something
and does not know how to do it best in the spirit of the forge; a help
on steroids, `/howto`, that takes the need in free words and answers
with the procedure or the method the forge has for it. Claude's
synthesis, not judged: the same mechanism as `/man` (POS.1190) with a
question in place of a name, so a third entry of `/man` and no command
of its own, `/howto` at most an alias as `/manual` is; the answer
names the procedure the forge has, a command, a method, a state of the
chain, a reviewer, a script, cited by the path of its definition and
never retold; where the forge has nothing, it says so and marks what
Claude would do without a mechanism as his own (POS.1370), which is
where threads of this intent are born; where several fit, one
recommended with its reason, once; nothing run and nothing written.
The risk: an answer better than the definitions, a procedure that
stands in no file; the citation by path is the guard, as it is for
`/man` today. The work: a third paragraph of the skill `man`, the row
of the commands table, a sentence in POS.1190. Opened 2026-10-15;
origin: the principal's word and Claude's synthesis.
