# Forge of Thought
*A workshop where thought is tempered and shaped.*

## What it is
Forge of Thought is a framework for structured, versioned documents
that take an idea from a rough brief to a worked-out intent and on to
what is handed to other people. The AI draws out what I have not yet
put into words, criticises it through isolated reviewers that never
see our conversation, and holds the structure and the order. The
person decides: every decision about content is his, and he answers
for what goes out.

## What it changes in daily work
- Whoever formulates an assignment is interviewed instead of facing
  an empty page: the AI asks, says at once what does not fit, and
  never fills a gap by assumption.
- Whoever receives an assignment gets it complete and precise, with
  nothing left out in silence, so that he can act without the author
  in the room and knows the reason well enough to act rightly where
  the plan no longer fits.
- The thinking is challenged before it is handed over, while a change
  is still cheap, and nothing lives only in a chat: the current state
  and every decision with its reason are in files, so work is picked
  up after weeks without digging through old conversations.
- Productivity and quality rise together because the AI is a
  cognitive extension, an amplifier of thinking and never its
  substitute: a human stays the author, fully responsible for the
  output. As the chain grows, the same reaches business analysts,
  UX/UI and test analysts.
- Common standards not only of the output but of its quality: for
  example an assignment analysed against shared security
  requirements, so that every project gets a consistent result.
  Shared material through libraries is there today; shared reviewers
  come with the user extensions named below.

## Where it works today, and where it goes
Two pieces of work in one company were written with the forge: the
building of an agentic platform, the company's one governed way to
run AI and automation in production, and the transformation of an IT
department into one that works with AI by design. Both are already
handed over to the people who take them further, and on both, teams
work with the forge themselves. I use it daily for further work of my
own, an AI strategy among it.

In that company the forge is to be a foundational building block of a
large part of the IT transformation ahead. Today a handful of people
work with it; after the next feature, described below, it should be
ten to twenty, and the end game is about fifty people who will use it
daily as their main tool.

## Where it honestly stands
- Today the chain runs from the brief and the intent to an assignment
  and a solution design.
- A BRD layer is next and is not built yet.
- A modified variant of the forge for test analysts and UX/UI is at
  the plan stage.
- User extensions - one's own reviewer, say a challenger, kept and
  developed in one's personal repository - are what has to be
  finished before a wider rollout.
- The main limitation today: one person works one document; sharing
  across projects through libraries works. There is long work ahead.
