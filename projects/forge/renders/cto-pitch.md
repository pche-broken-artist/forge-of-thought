# Forge of Thought
*A workshop where thought is tempered and shaped.*

## What it is
Forge of Thought is a framework for turning a raw idea into a chain
of structured, versioned documents, from the idea as it was first
written down to an assignment that others can take further. An AI
works as the author's cognitive extension: it interviews, elicits
what has not yet been articulated, criticises, and holds the
structure and the record; isolated reviewers that never see the
working conversation put adversarial pressure on the result. The
person decides everything about the content and stays fully
responsible for what leaves the workshop.

## What it changes in daily work
- Whoever formulates an assignment today gets a partner that asks
  the questions he would otherwise be asked weeks later, keeps every
  position and every rejected direction on the record, and lets him
  compose rather than draft from a blank page.
- Whoever receives an assignment gets one self-contained document
  with stable, identified items and the reasons behind them, instead
  of a chain of meetings, mails and half-remembered decisions.
- As the chain grows downward, business analysts, UX/UI and test
  analysts work the same way on their own layer, each derived from
  the one above, so a requirement can be traced back to the intent
  it came from and forward to what was built and tested from it.
- The gain is in both productivity and quality: the AI carries the
  structure, the hygiene and the second reading, the human carries
  the thinking and the responsibility, and a great deal more of his
  time goes into the substance.
- Standards become shared not only for the shape of the output but
  for its quality: an assignment analysed against shared security
  requirements gives every project a consistent result. Sharing
  material across projects through libraries works today; shared
  reviewers arrive with the user extensions described below.

## Where it works today, and where it goes
Two pieces of work in one company were written with the forge, one
the building of an agentic platform, the company's one governed way
to run AI and automation in production, the other the transformation
of an IT department into one that works with AI by design; both are
already handed over to the people who take them further, and on both
the teams now work with the forge themselves, while I use it daily
for my further work, an AI strategy among it.

In that company the forge is a foundational building block of a
large part of the IT transformation ahead: today a handful of people
work with it, after the next feature it will be ten to twenty, and
the end game is about fifty people using it daily as their main
tool.

## Where it honestly stands
- Today the chain ends at the intent and the assignment.
- A BRD layer is in development and testing.
- A modified variant of the forge for test analysts and UX/UI is at
  the plan stage.
- User extensions, one's own reviewer, say a challenger, kept and
  developed in one's personal repository, are what has to be
  finished before a wider rollout.
- The main limitation today: one person works one document; sharing
  across projects through libraries works. There is long work ahead.
