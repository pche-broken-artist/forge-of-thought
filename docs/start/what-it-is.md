# What Forge of Thought is

Forge of Thought is a workshop in which a thought is tempered into a
chain of versioned documents. This page says what that means, in one
paragraph, for someone who has not installed anything yet and wants
to know whether to read on: a person who would use it, or one who
wants to judge it.

## What it is

Forge of Thought is a place for forging thoughts. A thought of any
kind, a process redesign, a platform initiative, an organisational
topic, anything at all, travels a chain of versioned documents from a
rough idea onward. Along the way it is reviewed by agents that never
saw the conversation in which it was made. The forge runs in Claude
Code, Anthropic's agent for the command line: the slash commands,
the skills, the agents and the hooks of Claude Code are the forge's
mechanisms, and the forge is what they are told. The person who uses
it is the principal, that is, the one whose thinking is being forged.
The principal supplies the ideas, the answers and the decisions and
is the final authority on all content. Claude, the model behind the
agent, is the principal's cognitive extension: it owns structure,
order and process discipline, and it proposes but never decides. The
name says what the material is: thoughts are the raw material, with
nothing presumed about them, and forged documents are the product.
In daily speech it is simply "the Forge".

## How it works

One person starts from a rough brief, a first statement of what is
on his mind. Claude works it with him into an intent, the document
that holds what he actually holds to be true and wants, by asking
questions and taking his answers. The intent is the trunk of every
project. Below it a project takes the layers it needs, such as an
assignment, a business requirements document, user stories or a
solution design, and many projects end at the intent. At any point
the principal has the thinking attacked by reviewers that run
isolated from the conversation, each from a different angle, and
settles what they found. From the documents of the chain, outputs
for every audience are generated: a pitch, a summary, a picture, a
README. The documents stay the source of truth, and the outputs are
remade from them. A run of the forge ends where its owner is
satisfied: the outcome is a document the principal stands behind,
nothing further.

Technically, Forge of Thought is a git repository of slash commands,
isolated agents, templates, scripts and conventions, bound to no
domain: the same machinery that forges a platform assignment would
forge a role-playing campaign, with an intent of the story and
materials rendered for the game master.

## Why

The chain ends where the project needs it to, so that a project is
never forced through layers it has no use for. The name says thought
and not assignment for the same reason: an assignment is one place a
chain may end, not its goal. The reviewers are deliberately blind to
the working conversation, so that they bring adversarial pressure
from outside it rather than echo what was agreed inside it. Claude
proposes and never decides, because every decision about content
remains the principal's. Why the forge runs in Claude Code rather
than elsewhere is not recorded.
