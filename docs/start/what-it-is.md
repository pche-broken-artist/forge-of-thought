# About what the forge is

This page says in one paragraph what Forge of Thought is, for someone
who has just arrived and wants to know whether it is for them, or who
is weighing it up before anyone installs it. It was put together from
the forge's core rules and from the forge's own intent; it condenses
and adds nothing.

## What it is

Forge of Thought is a workshop where a thought is tempered and shaped:
an idea, of whatever kind, travels a chain of versioned documents
under isolated adversarial review, and comes out as something its
owner stands behind. It runs in Claude Code. The person whose
thinking is being forged is the principal: he supplies the ideas,
the answers and the decisions, and every decision about content is
his. Claude is his cognitive extension, an amplifier of his thinking
and never its substitute: it keeps structure, order and discipline,
it criticises, challenges, inspires and lays out options, and it
proposes but never decides; nothing enters the content because Claude
proposed it, only because the principal took it up. Isolated
reviewers, deliberately blind to the working conversation, press on
the thinking from different angles. The chain starts at a brief, its
trunk is the intent, and it ends where the project needs it to: below
the intent a project takes the layers it needs, and many end at the
intent. A forge run ends where its owner is satisfied; what comes out
is an artefact the principal stands behind, and the outputs meant for
an audience, a pitch, a summary, a picture, are generated from that
artefact rather than written beside it. Nothing is implemented here;
the forge specifies.

Technically, the forge is a git repository of slash commands, isolated
agents, templates, scripts and conventions, and the subject matter is
deliberately unconstrained: process redesign, platform initiatives,
organisational topics, a story for a game, anything.
