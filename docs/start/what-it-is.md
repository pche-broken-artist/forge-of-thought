# About what the forge is

This page is for someone meeting Forge of Thought for the first time,
whether to use it or to judge whether it is worth using. It says in
one paragraph what the forge is. It was put together from the forge's
operating rules and from its own intent: the identity, the roles, the
name and the end of a forge run as those two files state them.

## What it is

Forge of Thought is a place for forging thoughts: a workshop where
thought is tempered and shaped. An idea travels a chain of versioned
documents, from the idea put together onward, under isolated
adversarial review: AI reviewers, deliberately blind to the working
conversation, press on it from different angles. It runs in Claude
Code. You are the principal: whoever's thinking is being forged, the
one who supplies ideas, answers and decisions and has the final word
on all content. Claude is your cognitive extension, an amplifier of
your thinking and never its substitute: it owns structure, order and
process discipline, and it criticises, challenges, inspires and lays
out options, but proposes and never decides; nothing enters the
content because Claude proposed it, only because you took it up. The
chain starts at a brief and its trunk is the intent, and it ends
where the project needs it to: below the intent a project takes the
layers it needs, and many end at the intent. A forge run ends where
its owner is satisfied, and what comes out is an artefact the
principal stands behind, nothing further; outputs for a particular
audience, such as a pitch or an executive summary, are generated from
it, while the chain stays the source of truth. Technically the forge
is a git repository of slash commands, isolated agents, templates
and conventions, and its subject matter is deliberately
unconstrained: process redesign, platform initiatives, organisational
topics, or anything else.
