# Where the forge is going

The forge is developed in a direction, not towards a destination:
the chain of artefacts is extended downward as far as the principal
(the person whose thinking is forged) needs a thought taken, and no
layer, date or outcome is promised in advance. This page is for a
user who wants to know what to expect of the forge in the future, for
an extender who wants to know where the open places are, and for an
evaluator who wants to know what is deliberately left unpromised.
Terms of the forge used here are gathered in the
[Glossary](../reference/glossary.md).

## What it is

The forge is a general tool for forging thoughts. A forge run ends
where its owner is satisfied: the outcome is an artefact the principal
stands behind, and nothing further. An intent may be immaterial and
carry no delivery at all. The mechanics of the chain are
domain-agnostic: the same machinery that forges a platform assignment
would forge a campaign for a tabletop game, an intent of the story
and then materials rendered for the game master.

Sponsorship, funding, adoption and delivery outcomes are outside the
forge's scope and outside its sight. The door is kept open, not
closed: the forge may one day feed a delivery chain. Whether such a
delivery side would be grown layers of the forge or a separate
framework is deliberately undecided. Experience from use flows back
through the ordinary door, that is, the principal iterating the
intent, as input like any other.

The forge is developed at its owner's discretion and pace, by his
needs and by the feedback its use returns. A direction may be named;
destinations and deadlines are not.

## How it works

### The chain grows downward

The principal intends to keep extending the engine downward, so that
thoughts are forged as far as he needs them taken. Where the chain
stands today and how a layer is added to it is described in
[The document chain](the-document-chain.md). Of the layers below the
assignment:

- the BRD layer exists;
- an integration layer is intended;
- a strategy layer is possible, if it proves to make sense.

Which layers are added, and in what order, is open: possibly all of
these, possibly none yet. Nothing is approved for construction. The
mechanics of a layer (its commands, its agents, the calibration of its
reviewers) are designed when that layer is actually taken up, not in
advance; the solution design was made in the same way.

The layers below the assignment grow in the same project, by the same
principal's hand, when he chooses to take his own thought further: a
BRD is his next layer, not someone else's deliverable. What a
recipient does with an assignment in his own instance (his own
installation of the forge) is his own forge run: the assignment
becomes his brief, by his hand today. A command for that hand-over
would come only when that day comes. The chain never spans two
principals.

### A new kind of artefact

A new kind of artefact is added to the forge by one command,
`/new-artefact <name>`, so that the chain grows without anyone having
to know by heart what a kind of artefact is made of. The command leads
to everything a kind needs and forgets none of it:

- what the artefact is, for whom, from what it is derived and why,
  held as a position of the forge intent before anything is built;
- its definition (the state file that says how the artefact is
  found) and its template (the skeleton of what comes out), as a
  pair;
- the prefixes of its items, where it needs its own;
- whether it needs a challenger of its own, and what the critic must
  know of it;
- where it is realised, in the solution design.

The command decides nothing: how the kind is composed is the
principal's choice, as for any artefact, and each of these is born on
his word. The first artefact of the new kind is not the command's. It
is made through `/forge <name>` on real work, and that is the trial of
the definition. Where the kind needs a trial on real work before its
position can hold, the order is: the brief, then the pair of
definition and template built with its reviewers and tried, then the
position and the item of the design. The BRD layer was born in this
order.

For now the command adds to the engine, for every user of it. A user
is to be able to add an artefact of his own as easily, kept at his own
place; where that place is, is open. Types other than an artefact,
such as a whole chain, a reviewer or a genre, are not this command's.

### Reviewers on another model family

A model of another family is reached today for research; the link as
it stands is described in
[Another model family](another-model-family.md). Reviewers on that
link are intended and not built: a challenger persona and a critic
lens may be sent to another engine, so that `/challenge` can send in a
lens with genuinely different priors. What is composed is the forge's
own persona and contract. The engine reads the project from disk as a
subagent does (an agent run in isolation, which sees nothing of the
working conversation). The report is scanned for instance values and
checked for its shape before the command numbers and files it
unchanged. The engine is named at the call; several engines are
intended, without priority among them. The direction holds; the
mechanics are built for the research, and the reviewers on the link
are not yet built.

### What the forge wants of those who use it

The forge is open to those who use it. What it wants of them most is
feedback and ideas. A finished change is welcome too, on the forge's
own rule: a change of how the forge behaves goes through the chain
before it is built; a document that is a render is changed through its
recipe; and nobody sends a change he has not tried himself. How a
change reaches the forge is described in
[Make a change to the forge](../extend/how-a-change-is-made.md). A
visitor is told how, where the readers of GitHub look for it. What
enters the forge stays the principal's decision.

## Why

The chain is extended downward because the principal forges his
thoughts as far as he needs them taken; the forge is a general tool
and the run ends where its owner is satisfied. Why the mechanics of a
layer are designed only when the layer is taken up, and not in
advance, is not recorded beyond the precedent of the solution design.

A new kind of artefact is added by one command because a kind added by
hand took a search for everything it touches, and two of the things
it needs, a prefix and a challenger, are easily forgotten.

Reviewers on another model family are intended so that a challenge
can be made with genuinely different priors. Why they are not yet
built, beyond the fact that the mechanics so far serve the research,
is not recorded.

Why the chain never spans two principals, and why a recipient's work
on an assignment is a forge run of his own, is not recorded.

Why the forge is developed at its owner's discretion and pace, and why
feedback and ideas are wanted most, is not recorded.
