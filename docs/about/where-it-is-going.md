# Where the forge is going

The forge has a direction and no destination: it is extended
downward as far as the principal (the person whose thinking is
forged) needs a thought taken, at its owner's discretion and pace,
and it promises no layer, no feature and no date. This page is for
a user who wants to know what to expect, for an extender who wants
to know where the open places are, and for an evaluator who wants to
know what is deliberately not promised.

## What it is

The forge is a general tool for forging thoughts. A run of the forge
ends where its owner is satisfied: the outcome is an artefact the
principal stands behind, and nothing further. An intent may be
immaterial and carry no delivery at all. The mechanics of the
[chain](the-document-chain.md) are domain-agnostic: the same
machinery that forges a platform assignment would forge the story
of a role-playing campaign, an intent of the story and then
materials rendered for the game master.

Sponsorship, funding, adoption and delivery outcomes are outside the
forge's scope and outside its sight. The door to them is left open,
not closed: the forge may one day feed a delivery chain, and
experience from use flows back through the ordinary door, the
principal and `/forge intent`, as input like any other. Whether a
delivery side would be grown layers of the forge or a separate
framework is deliberately undecided.

The forge itself is developed by its owner's needs and by the
feedback its use returns. A direction may be named; destinations and
deadlines are not.

## How it works

### The chain grows downward

The chain is extended downward: a thought is forged as far as the
principal needs it taken. Today the chain has, below the intent, the
assignment, the BRD, the user stories and the solution design. The
user stories are an artefact of the chain, `35-user-stories.md`,
below the BRD and above the solution design: the parent broken into
behaviour seen from the outside, so that the stakeholders approve
them as a whole, the delivery builds from them and the testers write
their test cases from them. How a layer is added is said on
[The document chain](the-document-chain.md).

Beyond what exists, integration is intended, and a strategy layer is
possible if it proves to make sense. Which layers are added, and in
what order, is open: possibly all of these, possibly none yet.
Nothing is approved for construction. The mechanics of a layer, its
commands, its agents and the calibration of its reviewers, are
designed when that layer is actually taken up, not in advance. The
precedent is the solution design, whose mechanics were designed that
way.

The layers below the assignment grow in the same project, by the same
principal's hand, when he chooses to take his own thought further: a
BRD is his next layer, not someone else's deliverable. What a
recipient does with an assignment is his own forge run, in an
instance of his own. The assignment becomes his brief, by his hand
today. A command of its own for that step comes only when that day
comes. The chain never spans two principals.

### A new kind of artefact is one command

A new kind of artefact is added to the forge by one command,
`/new-artefact <name>`. The command leads to everything a kind needs
and forgets none of it:

- what the artefact is, for whom, from what it is derived and why,
  held as a position of the forge intent before anything is built;
- its definition and its template as a pair;
- the prefixes of its items, where it needs its own;
- whether it needs a challenger of its own, and what the critic must
  know of it;
- where it is realised, in the solution design.

The command decides nothing: how the kind is composed is the
principal's choice, as for any artefact, and each of these is born on
his word. The first artefact of the new kind is not the command's.
It is made through `/forge <name>` on real work, and that is the
trial of the definition. Where the kind needs a trial on real work
before its position can hold, the order is: the brief, the pair built
with its reviewers and tried, then the position and the item of the
design.

For now the command adds to the engine, for every user of it. A user
is to be able to add an artefact of his own as easily, kept at his
own place. Where that place is, is still open. Types other than an
artefact, a whole chain, a reviewer or a genre, are not this
command's. How the engine and the projects stand today is said on
[Projects and the engine](projects-and-the-engine.md).

### Reviewers on another model family

A model of another family is reached today for research, over the
link described on [Another model family](another-model-family.md).
A challenger persona and a critic lens may be sent over the same
link. What is composed is the forge's own persona and contract; the
other engine reads the project from disk as a subagent (an isolated
run of Claude that never sees the conversation) does; the report is
scanned for instance values and checked for its shape before the
command numbers and files it unchanged. The engine is named at the
call. The direction holds. The mechanics are built for the
research; the reviewers on the link are not yet built.

### Open to those who use it

The forge is open to those who use it. What it wants of them most is
feedback and ideas. A finished change is welcome too, on the forge's
own rule:

- a change of how the forge behaves goes through the chain before it
  is built;
- a document that is a render changes through its recipe;
- nobody sends a change he has not tried himself.

A visitor is told how, where the readers of GitHub look for it; the
way is [Make a change to the forge](../extend/how-a-change-is-made.md).
What enters the forge stays the principal's decision.

## Why

The chain grows downward, layer by layer, so that a thought is taken
exactly as far as the principal needs it and no further. A layer's
mechanics are designed when it is taken up. Because the solution
design was made that way, and the forge keeps to that precedent; a
fuller reason is not recorded.

A new kind of artefact is added by one command. Because a kind added
by hand took a search for everything it touches, and two of the
things it needs, a prefix and a challenger, are easily forgotten. So
that the chain grows without anyone having to know by heart what a
kind of artefact is made of.

A challenger and a critic lens are to be sent to another model
family. So that `/challenge` can send in a lens with genuinely
different priors. Why the reviewers on the link are not yet built is
not recorded.

The chain never spans two principals. The reason is not recorded.

The forge is developed at its owner's discretion and pace, with
direction named and no destination. The reason is not recorded
beyond what the scope says: a forge run ends where its owner is
satisfied, and sponsorship, funding, adoption and delivery are
outside its sight.

Feedback and ideas are what the forge wants most of those who use
it. The reason is not recorded. The rule for a finished change is the
forge's own rule for every change of itself: through the chain before
it is built, and tried before it is sent.
