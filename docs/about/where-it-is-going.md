# About where the forge is going

This page says in which direction the forge is being taken and what
is deliberately left open. It is for anyone who uses the forge,
extends it or judges it and wants to know what is planned, what is
only intended and what is not decided at all. It was put together
from the positions of the forge's own intent (`projects/forge/10-intent.md`);
it states direction as those positions state it, and nothing more.

## A direction, not a destination

The forge is a general tool for forging thoughts. A forge run ends
where its owner is satisfied: the outcome is an artefact the
principal stands behind, and nothing further. An intent may be
immaterial and carry no delivery at all; the machinery of the chain
is domain-agnostic, so the same mechanics that forge a platform
assignment would forge a game campaign. Sponsorship, funding,
adoption and what becomes of a delivery are outside the forge's
scope and outside its sight.

The forge itself is developed at its owner's discretion and pace, by
his needs and by the feedback its use returns. Direction may be
named; destinations and deadlines are not. What follows is therefore
a statement of where things point, not a schedule.

## The chain grows downward

The principal intends to keep extending the chain downward: a
thought is forged as far as he needs it taken. A BRD layer is certain
to come; integration is intended; a strategy layer is possible if it
proves to make sense. Which layers are added, and in what order, is
open: possibly all of these, possibly none yet.

Nothing is approved for construction. The mechanics of a layer, its
commands, its agents, the calibration of its reviewers, are designed
when that layer is actually taken up, not in advance; that is how the
solution design's were designed. The layers below the assignment grow
in the same project, by the same principal's hand, when he chooses to
take his own thought further: a BRD as his next layer, not as
someone else's deliverable. What a recipient does with an assignment
in his own instance is his own forge run, with the assignment as his
brief; the chain never spans two principals.

The door to a delivery side stays open rather than closed: the forge
may one day feed a delivery chain, and experience from use flows back
through the ordinary door, the principal iterating the intent, as
input like any other. Whether such a delivery side would be grown
layers of the forge or a separate framework is deliberately
undecided.

## A new kind of artefact by one command

A new kind of artefact is added to the forge by one command,
`/new-artefact <name>`, so that the chain grows without anyone having
to know by heart what a kind of artefact is made of. The command leads
to everything a kind needs and forgets none of it: what the artefact
is, for whom, from what it is derived and why, held as a position of
the forge intent before anything is built; its definition and its
template as a pair; the prefixes of its items where it needs its own;
whether it needs a challenger of its own and what the critic must know
of it; and where it is realised in the solution design. The command
decides nothing: each of these is born on the principal's word. The
first artefact of the new kind is not the command's; it is made
through `/forge <name>` on real work, and that is the trial of the
definition.

For now the command adds to the engine, for every user of it. A user
is to be able to add an artefact of his own as easily, kept at his own
place; where that place is remains open. Types other than an
artefact, such as a whole chain, a reviewer or a genre, are not this
command's.

The reason the command exists: a kind added by hand took a search for
everything it touches, and two of the things it needs, a prefix and a
challenger, are easily forgotten.

## Independent challengers

Challengers that run on the same model as the author share its
priors; a passed challenge then only means the artefact is consistent
under one set of priors. The calibration point lies outside, in
review by humans or by a different model family, invited at the
principal's discretion.

Independent challengers will therefore be built: challenger personas
running on a different model family than the author's, composed by
the principal outside the forge and invoked from it over a
command-line link, so that `/challenge` can send in a lens with
genuinely different priors. The direction is decided; the mechanics
are designed when taken up. Whatever such a challenger finds follows
the forge's ordinary rule: nothing enters the intent because a
reviewer wrote it, only because the principal composed it.

## One principal per instance

The forge stays a single-user tool per instance. More people means
more instances, with coordination left to git. Branches are
voluntary and belong to git; nothing forces one, and whoever does not
use them works on `main`, saves and now and then releases, and sees
none of this. How an assignment from one instance becomes the brief
of another is, for now, by the recipient's own hand; a command for it
comes only when that day comes.

## Open to those who use it

The forge is open to those who use it, and what it wants of them most
is feedback and ideas. A finished change is welcome too, on the
forge's own rule: a change of how the forge behaves goes through the
chain before it is built, a document that is a render goes through
its recipe, and nobody sends a change he has not tried himself. A
visitor is told how where the readers of a public repository look for
it. What enters the forge stays the principal's decision.
