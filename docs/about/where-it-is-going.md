# Where the forge is going

The forge has a direction of development and no destination: it
names which way it grows and promises neither what it will contain
nor when. This page is for anyone who uses the forge, extends it or
weighs whether to take it up, and wants to know what may come and
what is deliberately left unpromised.

## What it is

The direction is a set of intentions held by the principal (the
person whose thinking is forged, and who develops the forge itself).
None of them is approved for construction.

What is named:

- The chain grows downward: thoughts are forged as far as the
  principal needs them taken.
- A BRD layer is certain to come.
- Integration is intended.
- A strategy layer is possible, if it proves to make sense.
- Independent challengers are planned: challenger personas that run
  on a different model family than the one that wrote the artefact.
- A user is to be able to add a kind of artefact of his own, kept at
  his own place.

What is not promised:

- Which layers are added, and in what order. It may be all of the
  named ones, or none yet.
- Where a user's own kind of artefact will be kept. That place is
  open.
- Any destination or deadline.
- Any outcome beyond the forge itself. Sponsorship, funding, adoption
  and delivery lie outside its scope.

## How it works

**Layers are added when taken up.** The chain is the sequence of
versioned documents a thought travels, described in
[The document chain](the-document-chain.md). A new layer joins it
below the intent or the assignment. The mechanics of a layer (its
commands, its agents, the calibration of its reviewers) are designed
when that layer is actually taken up, not in advance. A layer below
the assignment grows in the same project, by the same principal's
hand, when he chooses to take his own thought further. A BRD, for
example, would be his next layer, not someone else's deliverable.

**One principal per chain.** The chain never spans two principals. A
recipient who receives an assignment works it in his own instance
(one installation of the forge): the assignment becomes his brief.
Today he makes it so by hand, which works because an assignment is
self-contained. A command of its own for this comes only when that
day comes.

**A new kind of artefact is one command.** `/new-artefact <name>`
leads through everything a kind of artefact needs:

- what the artefact is, for whom, from what it is derived and why,
  held as a position of the forge's own intent before anything is
  built;
- its definition and its template, as a pair;
- the prefixes of its items, where it needs its own;
- whether it needs a challenger of its own, and what the critic must
  know of it;
- where it is realised, in the solution design.

The command decides nothing. Each of these is born on the
principal's word, and how the kind is composed is his choice. The
first artefact of the new kind is not made by the command. It is
made through `/forge <name>` on real work, and that is the trial of
the definition. For now the command adds the kind to the engine, for
every user of it. Adding a kind at the user's own place is the open
part. Types other than an artefact are not this command's: a whole
chain, a reviewer and a genre among them.

**Independent challengers.** The direction is decided and the
mechanics are designed when taken up. The personas would be composed
by the principal and invoked from the forge, so that `/challenge` can
send in a lens running elsewhere.

**Pace and contributions.** The forge is developed at the
principal's discretion and pace, by his needs and by the feedback it
returns. What the forge wants most from those who use it is feedback
and ideas. A finished change is welcome too, on the forge's own rule:

- a change of how the forge behaves goes through the chain before it
  is built;
- a document that is a render changes through its recipe;
- nobody sends a change he has not tried himself.

How such a change reaches the forge is described in
[Make a change to the forge](../extend/how-a-change-is-made.md). What
enters the forge stays the principal's decision.

**The door to delivery stays open.** The forge may one day feed a
delivery chain. Experience from use then flows back the ordinary
way: through the principal, into the intent, as input like any
other. Whether a delivery side would be grown layers of the forge or
a separate framework is deliberately undecided.

## Why

A forge run ends where its owner is satisfied: the outcome is an
artefact the principal stands behind, nothing further. The forge is
a general tool, and its mechanics do not depend on the domain. The
same machinery that forges a platform assignment would forge the
story of a role-playing campaign and the materials rendered for it.
For this reason delivery outcomes are outside its sight, and a
direction may be named while destinations and deadlines are not.

`/new-artefact` exists because a kind of artefact added by hand took
a search for everything it touches. Two of the things a kind needs,
a prefix and a challenger, are easily forgotten. The command exists
so that the chain grows without anyone having to know by heart what
a kind of artefact is made of.

Challengers on another model family are planned so that a review can
bring genuinely different priors from those of the model that wrote
the artefact.

Why the mechanics of a layer wait until the layer is taken up: the
reason is not recorded. Why feedback and ideas are wanted above
finished changes: the reason is not recorded.
