# About where the forge is going

This page says in which direction the forge is being developed and
what is deliberately left open, for anyone who uses it, extends it
or is weighing it up. It was put together from the positions of the
forge's own intent, `projects/forge/10-intent.md`: only the
positions, because the open threads change with every round. It
states direction as the positions state it, and no more.

## A direction, not a destination

The forge is developed at its owner's discretion and pace, by his
needs and by what comes back from use. Direction may be named;
destinations and deadlines are not. Nothing on this page is a
promise of a release, and nothing is approved for construction
until it is taken up.

The forge itself is a general tool for forging thoughts, and a
forge run ends where its owner is satisfied: the outcome is an
artefact the principal stands behind, nothing further. Whether it
will one day feed a delivery chain, and whether that would be
grown layers of the forge or a separate framework, is deliberately
undecided. The door stays open: experience from use flows back
through the ordinary door, the intent, as input like any other.

## The chain grows downward

The chain is to be extended downward as far as the principal needs
a thought taken. A BRD layer is certain to come; solution
architecture and integration are intended; a strategy layer is
possible if it proves to make sense. Which layers are added, and in
what order, is open: possibly all of these, possibly none yet.

The mechanics of a layer, its commands, its agents and the
calibration of its reviewers, are designed when that layer is
actually taken up, not in advance.

The layers below the assignment grow in the same project, by the
same principal's hand, when he chooses to take his own thought
further: a BRD as his next layer, not as someone else's
deliverable. What a recipient does with an assignment in his own
instance is his own forge run: the assignment becomes his brief, by
his own hand today, by a command of its own only when that day
comes. The chain never spans two principals.

How a layer is added is described in
[About the document chain](the-document-chain.md).

## New kinds of artefact

A new kind of artefact is added to the forge by one command,
`/new-artefact <name>`, so that the chain grows without anyone
having to know by heart what a kind of artefact is made of. The
command leads to everything a kind needs and forgets none of it:
what the artefact is, for whom, from what it is derived and why,
held as a position of the forge intent before anything is built;
its definition and its template as a pair; the prefixes of its
items where it needs its own; whether it needs a challenger of its
own and what the critic must know of it; and where it is realised,
in the solution design. The command decides nothing: each of these
is born on the principal's word. The first artefact of the new kind
is made through `/forge <name>` on real work, and that is the trial
of the definition.

For now the command adds to the engine, for every user of it. A
user is to be able to add an artefact of his own as easily, kept at
his own place; where that place is remains open. Types other than
an artefact, such as a whole chain, a reviewer or a genre, are not
this command's.

## Independent challengers

Independent challengers will be built: challenger personas running
on a different model family than the author's, so that `/challenge`
can send in a lens with genuinely different priors. The reason is
that agreement between reviewers on one model is never treated as
validation; it only means the artefact is consistent under one set
of priors, and the calibration point lies outside. The direction is
decided; the mechanics are designed when taken up.

## One principal per instance

The forge stays a single-user tool per instance. More people means
more instances and coordination by git. Branches are voluntary and
belong to git: whoever does not use them works on the released
line, saves and now and then releases, and sees none of it.

## What the forge wants from those who use it

The forge is open to those who use it. What it wants of them most
is feedback and ideas. A finished change is welcome too, on the
forge's own rule: a change of how the forge behaves goes through
the chain before it is built, a document that is a render goes
through its recipe, and nobody sends a change he has not tried
himself. What enters the forge stays the principal's decision.

How a change reaches the forge is described in
[Make a change to the forge](../extend/how-a-change-is-made.md).
