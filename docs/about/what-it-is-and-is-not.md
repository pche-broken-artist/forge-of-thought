# About what the forge is and is not

This page says what Forge of Thought is, what it deliberately is
not, and why it carries the name it does. It is for anyone meeting
the forge for the first time, whether to use it, to extend it or to
judge it. It was put together from the engine's core instructions
(`CLAUDE.md`) and the forge's own intent
(`projects/forge/10-intent.md`): the identity and the two roles from
the former, the reasons from the latter.

## What it is

Forge of Thought is a place for forging thoughts: a workshop where
thought is tempered and shaped. Any idea, whether a process
redesign, a platform initiative, an organisational topic or anything
else, travels a chain of versioned documents from the idea put
together onward, under isolated adversarial review. The subject
matter is deliberately unconstrained; nothing is presumed about the
thoughts that come in.

The forge is a general-purpose engine. It is not bound to one person
or one management relationship. It began as one person's tool, and
that origin is now an instance fact, not the system: it describes the
first instance, not the engine, and the founding framing was dropped
for exactly that reason. Who the principal of an instance is lives in
that instance's local configuration, never in the engine.

Two roles meet in it:

- The **principal** is whoever's thinking is being forged. He
  supplies ideas, answers and decisions and is the final authority on
  all content.
- The **recipients** are whoever receives the result: teams,
  colleagues, or the principal's future self.

Claude is the principal's cognitive extension, not a supplier. Claude
owns structure, order, process discipline and document hygiene; the
principal owns content and every decision. Claude's contribution to
content is to criticise, challenge, inspire and lay out options; the
composition is the principal's. Nothing enters the content because
Claude proposed it, only because the principal took it up. "Cognitive
extension" therefore means an amplifier of the principal's thinking,
never its substitute: Claude proposes and never decides. The two
roles and the rule between them are the matter of
[About the principal and Claude](the-principal-and-claude.md).

## What it is not

**Not a program.** Nothing is implemented in the forge; the engine
specifies. What comes out of it is documents: an intent the principal
stands behind, an assignment where a project has one, further layers
where a project needs them.

**Not a delivery tool.** A forge run ends where its owner is
satisfied: the outcome is an artefact the principal stands behind,
nothing further. An intent may be immaterial and carry no delivery at
all. Sponsorship, funding, adoption and delivery outcomes are outside
the forge's scope and outside its sight. The door is left open rather
than closed: the forge may one day feed a delivery chain, and what is
learned from use flows back through the ordinary door, the principal
working the intent, as input like any other. Whether a delivery side
would be grown layers of the forge or a separate framework is
deliberately undecided.

**Not a chat.** State lives in files, never only in a conversation. A
session can be ended at any point without loss, and the next one
re-orients from the project's ledger, the single source of truth for
state. Nothing depends on what a conversation remembers.

**Not tied to one domain.** The chain's mechanics are
domain-agnostic: the same machinery that forges a platform assignment
would forge the story of a role-playing campaign, as an intent of the
story and then materials rendered for the game master.

## Why the name

The system is named Forge of Thought because thoughts are the raw
material, of whatever kind, and forged artefacts are the product. The
name was chosen with the full chain in mind: from a raw idea, layer
by layer, as far as a project needs to go. That is why the name says
"thought" and not "assignment": the assignment is only where the
first version of the chain happened to end, and no subtitle or
one-line description of the forge may name the assignment as the
goal. The subtitle is "A workshop where thought is tempered and
shaped": any description must present the forge as a place where
thoughts are forged.

Other names were considered and rejected. "Cascade" literally names
the waterfall. "Foundry" collides with existing products.
"Continuum", "Strata" and "Idea Forge" were weighed; Forge of Thought
was chosen. In daily speech the short form "Forge" is expected; the
full name stands in documents.

## Why the chain ends where the project needs it to

The ambition is the full chain, growing layer by layer from a raw
idea toward a realisation-ready specification. But the chain is not a
fixed length: below the intent a project takes the layers it needs,
none a condition of another, and many projects end at the intent. A
layer a project does not have is not missing. The chain grows by
adding a layer's definition, without reworking anything that exists,
which is why files and IDs are numbered with gaps. How one thought
makes its way along that chain is told in
[About how a thought travels](how-a-thought-travels.md).
