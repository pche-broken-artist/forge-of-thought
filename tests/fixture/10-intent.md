# Fixture — Intent

## Essence
A lockable shed for six bicycles by the gate before the autumn. The
shed is built, not bought, because no bought shed fits the gate.

## Positions
- **POS.0010** The shed stands by the gate, nowhere else: the
  bicycles are taken out at the gate and brought back there.
- **POS.0020** The shed is built from wood by the house, to the
  width of the gate's wall, 3.2 metres.

## Facts
- **FCT.0010** The wall by the gate is 3.2 metres wide
  (sources/shed-minutes.md).

## Rejected directions
- **REJ.0010** A bought metal shed: none of the three offers fits the
  wall, each is wider than 3.2 metres.
